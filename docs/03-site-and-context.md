# Site & Context

## Location

**Paratwada–Chikhaldara Road, Amravati district, Maharashtra**

The project uses the Chikhaldara–Melghat landscape as a setting for a hospitality-led eco-tourism proposal.

## Contextual Characteristics

- hilly terrain;
- forested surroundings;
- scenic views;
- strong monsoon influence;
- cooler hill climate than surrounding plains;
- regional tourism identity;
- proximity to rural/tribal communities;
- potential agricultural linkages.

## Architectural Implications

The context suggested that the project should:

- remain low-rise;
- follow contours rather than impose a rigid grid;
- minimise large retaining structures;
- preserve drainage lines and existing vegetation;
- use protected openings and deep overhangs;
- create a strong indoor–outdoor relationship;
- keep the architecture visually recessive in the landscape.

## Site Planning Principle

The site is treated as a **landscape first, development second**.

The broad planning logic is:

1. identify topography, drainage and vegetation;
2. define sensitive/no-build areas;
3. identify suitable development pockets;
4. organise arrival and service zones near the road;
5. distribute low-rise stay clusters;
6. connect them through pedestrian paths and nature trails;
7. retain ecological continuity between built areas.
